# Phase 3b - Non-Coherent MUSIC Validation on the 1.9 Lambda NULA

A focused validation of MUSIC on the sparse NULA [0,1,4,8] with 1.9 lambda spacing and non-coherent sources.

Experimental conditions:
- Array: 1D sparse NULA [0, 1, 4, 8]
- Physical spacing: 1.9 lambda
- Sources: 2 non-coherent narrowband targets
- Snapshots: T = 200
- FOV: [-15 deg, 15 deg]
- Minimum separation: 5 deg
- Method: MUSIC

| Algorithm | RMSE (deg) |
| --- | ---: |
| MUSIC | MUSIC | 5.3576 |
